Welcome! 👋

Thanks for installing Save Website as Image. It captures the whole page, not just what is visible, with a single click.

  1. Open the page you want to save.
  2. Click the camera button in the toolbar.
  3. The image is saved to your downloads folder. A shutter sound confirms the capture (you can change or mute it below).

To capture several pages at once, select the tabs first (Ctrl/Cmd+click or Shift+click on the tabs) and then click the toolbar button. Every selected tab is saved as its own file.

The toolbar button shows ✓ or ✕ to tell you whether the last capture of the current page worked. Hover over it for details.

Very tall pages can exceed the browser's canvas limit. In that case the page is saved as a series of images in a .cbz zip archive instead.
